Mapped the chemical space of 1,971 human GLP1r agonists (CHEMBL1784) in DataWarrior, plotting Molecular Weight against lipophilicity (cLogP)

The distribution clearly separates two distinct medicinal chemistry approaches:

Non-peptide small-molecule agonists: Cluster at lower molecular weights with positive cLogP values (0 to +10),
designed to cross cell membranes passively for oral bioavailability.

Peptide and lipidated peptide agonists: Extend across higher molecular weights with strongly negative cLogP values (-10 to -40)
driven by polar amide backbones, requiring fatty acid lipidation or permeation enhancers like SNAC for clinical delivery
